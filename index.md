I’m a fifth-year PhD (DPhil) candidate in Finance at the University of Oxford. My research sits at the intersection of innovation, public policy, and finance. In particular, I focus on the design of innovation and science public policy, entrepreneurial finance in deep-tech, and how AI changes knowledge production. I hold graduate degrees in Economics from the London School of Economics and the University of Wisconsin-Madison. Previously, I worked in corporate tax policy.
